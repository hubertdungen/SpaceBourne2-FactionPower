# Changelog

## 0.1.1 — 2026-10-31

- After loading a save, the mod follows the live game world (a previous world left in memory could
  still look valid).
- **On by default** (the realism set is installed ready to play); Shift+F9 still switches it off.
- README and Nexus page: links to every mod of the realism set (SB2 HOTAS added); 16:9 cover image.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-31

First release.

- Missions (contracts, bounties, guild and side quests) pay Faction Power by type and
  difficulty; story missions and your faction's mail operations are left to vanilla.
- Dungeons (boss floor cleared) and strategic shop sales pay Faction Power.
- The ruling family of the system gains or loses local support (a "Local support" line in the
  game's dominance) and relation with your faction, depending on the deed.
- Diminishing returns: market saturation, 2 000 Power per game day, influence cap and fading,
  daily budgets, no repeats.
- Shift+F9 switches the mod on/off (off by default) and shows the local balance.
- Own ledger per character with rollback when an older save is loaded; cleanup option.
